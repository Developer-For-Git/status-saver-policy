Privacy Policy for Status Saver & Story Download

Last updated: November 14, 2026
Developer: Snapkeep | Contact: developerforgit2@gmail.com
  1. What the app does: The app lets you view photos and videos from statuses you have already viewed on your device, and save copies to your phone's gallery.
  2. Information we collect: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no account or sign-in.
  3. How the app uses your files: The app asks you to select the status folder on your device. It reads files in that folder only on your phone, to show them to you and to save the ones you choose. Your files are not uploaded to any server.
  4. Permissions: The app uses only the folder access you grant through Android's file picker. You can remove this access at any time in Android settings or by clearing the app's data.
  5. Third parties: The app is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
  6. Children: The app is not directed at children under 13.
  7. Changes: If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
  8. Contact: developerforgit2@gmail.com
